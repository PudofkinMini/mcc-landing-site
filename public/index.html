Route accounting

From load sheet to invoice.

  1. At the plant

    Build the load.

    Use each customer’s order to prepare the carts. Check the load weight before the truck leaves the dock.

  2. At the stop

    Record the exchange.

    Drivers record clean deliveries and soiled returns, adjust quantities and collect a signature at the customer’s door.

  3. In the office

    Settle the route.

    Review delivery adjustments and invoice each account on its agreed terms, whether by piece, weight or flat rate.

Inventory management

About MCC

Our roots are
in the field.

Mobile Computing Corp. is based in Mississauga, Ontario. We’ve built software for field operations since 1986. Today, our focus is commercial laundry and textile rental.

Our work in equipment rental, HVAC service and medical waste collection taught us how to track assets between stops and document each handoff. We bring that experience to the linen route.
